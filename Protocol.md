# Liquidity Partner & Treasury Allocation Philosophy

## Liquidity Partnership Without Forced FDV Dependence

The liquidity partner relationship is structured to provide market support and ecosystem flexibility without requiring the remaining equity position to be used solely as a mechanism to target a specific Fully Diluted Valuation (FDV).

The objective is flexibility.

The company retains the ability to allocate resources toward productive causes that strengthen the underlying business, ecosystem, and long-term value creation.

---

## Asset Allocation Freedom

The protocol maintains the freedom to utilise up to **51% of assets** toward productive ecosystem initiatives.

This allows a balanced approach between:

* Operational growth
* Strategic investments
* Liquidity commitments
* Treasury preservation
* Long-term asset accumulation

The focus remains on building underlying value rather than relying purely on token market structure.

---

## Holdings, Savings & Underlying Value Ratio

The treasury model considers three primary components:

1. **Holdings** — ecosystem and strategic asset positions
2. **Savings** — retained reserves and financial stability
3. **Underlying** — business value, revenue generation, and productive assets

Example target allocation reference:

**£3.28 million : £820K : £320K**

## Long-Term Principle

Liquidity provides access.

Treasury discipline creates resilience.

Underlying growth creates lasting value.

The objective is to ensure that token economics, company assets, and productive business activity remain aligned over time.
